# Skills

Skills for Claude that I use and share. Each folder holds one skill as a `SKILL.md`.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [substack-lens-replies](substack-lens-replies/SKILL.md) | Daily Substack outreach, end to end: finds posts from your inbox and keyword search, comments with one real question through your project's lens, follows up on replies, hearts, drafts proposals, files notifications, keeps a tracker doc and emails a summary. |

## Using a skill

Download the skill's `SKILL.md` and add it to Claude as a skill, if your plan or organization allows custom skills. On its first run, `substack-lens-replies` asks for your name, link, lens and mailbox, and starts in draft-only mode: nothing is posted until you say so.
